Hi, I'm Christine.
the human behind the atelier.

In a world that rewards speed, I wanted to build something small, deliberate, and quietly luxurious. Every piece that leaves the studio is made by hand, in small batches, with attention to the details that most people never see.

For nearly two decades, I've been shaping hats — first as a hobby, then as an obsession. I keep choosing this craft because the women who wear our pieces keep choosing us back. Thank you for being here.

Christine's Atelier has been self-funded from the beginning. That independence means every decision is intentional — from the materials we source to the way we price our pieces.

We exist to advocate for thoughtful design, for skilled makers, and for headwear that feels like an extension of the person wearing it. Not a costume — a decision.

Our Concept

01

A small, edited collection of timeless, sculptural pieces — each designed to be worn for years, not seasons. Transparency, fair pricing, and careful sourcing are built into every step.

Our Materials

02

Straw, wool felt, silk, sinamay — sourced from small mills and family-run suppliers. Every material is chosen because it will hold its shape, age beautifully, and feel right on the head.

Our People

03

A small team of makers, pattern-cutters, and finishers working from a single studio in Lekki. Every hat passes through several pairs of hands before it reaches yours.

Slow Creation

04

Blocking, trimming, and finishing are done by hand. We embrace the pauses — the hours spent getting the angle of a brim exactly right. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is wasted.

August 2006 — The Idea

It started in a small back room in Lagos, with one block, one bundle of straw, and a stubborn idea that a hat could be more than an accessory — that it could be a decision, worn openly.

The first piece took weeks. The second took days. By the third, I knew this was the thing I wanted to spend my life doing.

Every collection since has started from that same place: a single sketch, a quiet morning, and the question of what the women who wear our hats actually need.

Twenty years on, the studio still works from that original intention — patient, deliberate, and quietly stubborn about doing things the long way.
